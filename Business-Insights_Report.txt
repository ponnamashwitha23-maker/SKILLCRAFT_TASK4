Business Insights Report (EDA)
Objective
The objective of this analysis was to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a marketing campaign dataset, clean the data, and identify factors that can improve marketing performance and return on investment (ROI).
Data Cleaning
Removed duplicate records.
Handled missing values.
Verified data types.
Exported the cleaned dataset as a new CSV file.
Key Findings
Campaign performance varies across advertisers.
Higher approved budgets generally lead to higher audience reach.
Some campaigns have high media costs but lower performance.
Optimizing budget allocation can improve overall ROI.
Recommendations
Increase budget for high-performing campaigns.
Reduce spending on low-performing campaigns.
Monitor campaign performance regularly.
Continue using data analysis for future marketing decisions.
Conclusion
The analysis shows that effective budget allocation and continuous monitoring of campaign performance can improve marketing efficiency and maximize ROI.
